How it works

From recording to evidence in four steps

AssureStream sits beside your contact centre platform. Nothing you run today has to be replaced.

01 Connect

Bring in conversations

Recordings, transcripts, chats and emails from your platform, by API, secure file transfer or an on-site edge agent.

02 Score

Apply your rules

Speech is transcribed, then each interaction is scored against your scorecards, with the quoted evidence for every result.

03 Act

Route to a person

Each flag becomes a Touchpoint ticket for the right unit, an item on the next meeting agenda, or a sealed case for the ethics office.

04 Govern

Keep humans accountable

Reviewers confirm or override AI scores, every change is logged, and reports export for audit and regulators.

Security and governance

Built for organisations that are audited

Conversations contain personal and financial data. These are the controls we build in, and how we describe them: precisely.

Tenant isolation in the database

Each client's data is separated by row-level security enforced in the database itself, not only in the application.

Encryption

Data is encrypted in transit with TLS and at rest by the hosting platform, in the region you choose.

Card data kept out

Card numbers, security codes and ID numbers are removed from transcripts before anything is stored.

No training on your data

Your conversations are processed to deliver your service and are never used to train shared models.

Role-based access and audit

Agents, supervisors, QA and compliance see only what their role allows; every view, score change and export is logged.

Sealed whistleblowing channel

Protected disclosures are encrypted with a key outside the database, readable only by appointed ethics officers, and held under legal hold.

Human oversight

AI scores are explainable and can be overridden; low-confidence results go to people by design.

Certifications. AssureStream is designed to help clients meet POPIA, GDPR and sector conduct rules. We are preparing for independent SOC 2 and ISO 27001 assessment and do not claim either certification yet. Our security documentation and data processing agreement are available under NDA.

Pilot

Prove it on your own conversations in eight weeks

A fixed-scope pilot with one team, measured against a baseline you agree up front. You decide on the results.

  1. Weeks 1 to 2

    Set up and baseline

    Connect a sample of recordings, encode your scorecard, measure today's QA coverage and results.

  2. Weeks 3 to 4

    Calibrate

    Compare AI scores with your QA team's on the same interactions and tune until they agree.

  3. Weeks 5 to 7

    Run live

    Score every interaction for the pilot team; supervisors use alerts and coaching views.

  4. Week 8

    Evaluate

    A results report against the agreed measures and a costed plan for roll-out, or a clean exit.

Questions

What buyers usually ask

How accurate are the AI scores?

Accuracy depends on your scorecard and audio quality, so we measure it on your own interactions during the pilot by comparing AI scores with your QA team's. Results below a confidence threshold you set go to a human reviewer instead of being reported automatically.

Do we have to change our contact centre platform?

No. AssureStream works beside your platform, using recordings, transcripts and your reporting data. Integrations that are not ready today are built during the pilot.

Where is our data stored, and who can see it?

In the region or on the servers you choose. Access is limited by role, every access is logged, and your data is never used to train shared models.

Which languages do you support?

Calls are transcribed and analysed in the language spoken, and reports follow your organisation's language. Transcription covers the languages supported by the speech provider used in your deployment.

Are you SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certified?

Not yet. We are preparing for independent assessment and share our security documentation, architecture and data processing agreement under NDA so your security team can review them.

How long until we see results?

The pilot runs for eight weeks, with the first scored interactions usually in the second week once recordings and your scorecard are connected.
